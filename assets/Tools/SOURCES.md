# Tool photo sources

These JPG photos are stored locally in this folder and are loaded by the inventory cards. Tool variants that share a product photo are grouped by the same photographed tool type.

## Layout images already present

The original photos in this folder were retained: 6-inch Scale, 12-inch Scale, Dividers, Compass, Rivet Spacer, Scribe, Solid & Hollow Punch Set, Prick Punch, Center Punch, Automatic Center Punch, Pin Punch Set, Drive Punch, Hammer, Hole Duplicator, Grip Gauge, and Circular-Cutting Saw.
